Features

A Smarter, More Connected Life

Watkins Home Companion is designed to bring together your devices, apps, and daily tasks in one intelligent assistant. Every feature is labelled with its real status.

In testing Works in internal test builds and is not publicly available yet In development Being built, not finished or verified Planned On the roadmap

Voice Commands

Speak naturally or type. WHC replies out loud, asks a short question when a request is unclear, and pauses in sleep mode until you wake it.

In testing

Windows App Control

Ask WHC to open apps and websites you have approved on your Windows PC. If a name is unclear, it asks instead of opening the wrong thing.

In testing

Smart Home Control

Connections to Home Assistant and Bluetooth devices, with clear labels showing what is connected and what is a demo. Not yet tested with real hardware.

In development

Personal Assistance

Capture tasks and complete them with Undo, set reminders and alarms, and see your day in the Today view.

In testing

Family Mode (Optional)

Choose it during setup to add household profiles and parent-controlled permissions. Personal Mode keeps your companion private to you.

In testing

Privacy & Permissions

Control Center lists exactly what WHC may do. Memories are saved only when you ask, and personal data stays on your device in current builds.

In testing

Memories & Routines

Ask WHC to remember a detail and recall it later, review your routines, and get a briefing that reports only real data.

In testing

Deeper App Control

Helping you get things done inside your applications, not just opening them, always with clear permission.

Planned

Accounts & Sync

Optional accounts for using WHC across devices. This needs an online service that hasn’t been built yet.

Planned
