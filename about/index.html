About WHC

Our Mission

Watkins Home Companion was created with a simple goal: to make technology work for you.

We believe your devices, apps, and home should be easier to control, safer to use, and more helpful in your everyday life. WHC is being developed to combine AI with a clean, intuitive design, giving you a personal companion that understands your needs.

Where we are now

Honest progress, shared openly.

WHC is in active development. Core personal-assistance features are working in our internal test builds on Windows desktop. Smart-home connections are being built and still need testing with real hardware.

Before public release, we're validating WHC with people who haven't used it before, testing on clean machines, and completing accessibility checks. We'll share more when it's ready, and not before.

Honest about capability

We label what's real, what's a demo, and what isn't built yet, in the product and on this website.

Privacy first

Explicit memories, visible permissions, and personal data kept on your device in current builds.

Made for the whole household

Voice is never the only way in, and Family Mode gives parents control over sensitive actions.
