Designed to earn trust

Helpful at home means careful at home.

These principles shape every part of WHC, from the first conversation to the last device it controls.

Shows before it acts

WHC shows what it understood before any sensitive action and waits for your go-ahead.

Asks instead of guessing

When a request is unclear, you get a short follow-up question, not a wrong guess.

Honest about what's real

An activity log shows what changed, and demo controls are always labelled as demos.

Remembers what you choose

Memories are saved only when you ask. In current builds, personal data stays on your device.

Where WHC stands today

Clear about what's ready and what isn't.
