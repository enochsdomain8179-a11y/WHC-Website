Common questions

Can I buy or subscribe to WHC today?

No. WHC is in active development and isn't available for purchase or subscription.

How much will WHC cost?

Prices haven't been set. When they are, they'll be published on this page.

Do I need an account?

There are no WHC accounts today. Optional accounts and sync are planned, and they depend on an online service that hasn't been built yet.

Is anyone asking for payment on WHC's behalf?

WHC isn't taking payments of any kind. If someone asks you to pay for WHC, it isn't us. Please let us know.
