How It Works

Simple. Powerful. Built for You.

Watkins Home Companion is designed to fit into your life. Here's how it works in our current test builds. Details may change before release.

  1. 1

    Set It Up

    Choose your name for WHC, pick Personal or Family Mode, and decide what it's allowed to do.

  2. 2

    Give a Command

    Speak naturally or type. Both work the same way, so use whichever suits the moment.

  3. 3

    See It in Action

    WHC confirms what it understood, asks if anything is unclear, and checks before sensitive actions.

  4. 4

    Stay in Control

    An activity log shows what WHC changed and whether it was real or a demo.

You“Hey WHC, remind me to call Dad at 6 PM.”

WHC“Got it. I’ll remind you at 6:00 PM today.”

Privacy by design

You decide what your companion keeps.

Privacy isn't a setting we added later. It's part of how WHC is built.

Stored on your device

In current test builds, your tasks, reminders, and memories are stored locally on your computer.

Explicit memories only

WHC saves a detail only when you ask it to. It doesn't build a profile of you in the background.

Permissions you can see

Control Center lists exactly which actions WHC may take, and Family Mode adds parent-controlled permissions.
